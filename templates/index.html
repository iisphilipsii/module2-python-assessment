About The Club

Founded on 3 June 1892, Liverpool FC has played at Anfield since its very first season. The club grew into a European powerhouse under Bill Shankly and Bob Paisley in the 1960s, 70s and 80s, a period that delivered four European Cups and a run of league titles that set the standard for English football.

The modern era brought a sixth European Cup in Madrid in 2019 and a long-awaited league title the following season. Anfield remains one of the great atmospheres in world sport, with the Kop singing "You'll Never Walk Alone" before every home game — a tradition that has been part of the club since the 1960s.
